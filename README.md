# Electric-Vehicle-EV-Market-Performance-Analysis
Electric Vehicle Market &amp; Performance Analysis using Python, Power BI, and Tableau to explore EV range, battery capacity, efficiency, charging performance, and market trends.
----

**Project Objective**
The main objective of this project is to provide a structured, data-driven analysis of modern electric vehicles by examining performance, battery technology, charging capabilities, drivetrain configurations, vehicle segments, and market trends. 

**#What the Project Does**
This project analyzes a dataset of 478 electric vehicle models with 22 technical attributes to understand EV performance and market characteristics.

**The analysis focuses on:**
1) Which EV models have the highest driving range?
2) How does battery capacity affect driving range?
3) How are battery capacity and fast-charging power related?
4) Which brands and models lead in efficiency and performance?
5) How do FWD, RWD, and AWD vehicles differ in performance?
6) Which vehicle body types and market segments dominate the EV market?


**Why This Project Is Useful**
The project provides a data-driven understanding of the current EV landscape and helps users compare vehicles across important technical and market indicators.

It can be useful for:
**Consumers** comparing EV specifications and performance
**Automotive businesses** benchmarking EV models
**Researchers** studying electric mobility trends
**Data analysts** exploring relationships between technical variables
**Manufacturers** understanding performance and market segments
The analysis found a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.88 between battery capacity and driving range, showing the importance of energy storage for extended range.


**Tech Stack & Tools**
**Programming Language:** Python
**Data Cleaning & Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
**Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
**Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI
**Interactive Visualization:** Tableau
**Data Source:** EV specifications dataset containing 478 vehicle models
The project follows a complete workflow from data collection and inspection through cleaning, exploratory data analysis, correlation analysis, and visualization.



**Key Insights**

**Driving Range:** Mercedes-Benz EQS 450+ records the highest driving range at 685 km.
**Top Speed:** Maserati GranTurismo Folgore reaches 325 km/h.
**Battery & Range:** Battery capacity has a strong positive correlation with driving range (0.8804).
**Fast Charging:** Battery capacity also has a positive relationship with fast-charging power (0.7286).
**Drivetrain:** AWD vehicles have the highest average driving range, top speed, and acceleration among the analyzed drivetrain categories.
**Body Type:** SUVs dominate the dataset with 244 models.
**Charging Standard:** CCS is used by approximately 99.8% of the vehicles in the dataset.
